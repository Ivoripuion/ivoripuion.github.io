That fall, all that the Mission talked about was control: arms control, information control, resources control, psycho-political control, population control, control of the almost supernatural inflation, control of terrain through the Strategy of the Periphery. But when the talk had passed, the only thing left standing up that looked true was your sense of how out of control things really were. ——Michael Herr

那个秋天，代表团谈论的一切都是控制：军控、信息控制、资源控制、心理-政治控制、人口控制、对那近乎超自然通胀的控制、以及通过边缘策略对地形的控制。但当这些交谈平息后，唯一依然屹立、显得真实的东西，便是你对事物究竟有多么失控的真切感知。——Michael Herr

note：象征化越严重，实在界的裂隙就越会凸显，也正如简单反抗其实不如过度服从。

Conrad’s Heart of Darkness becomes Apocalypse Now. In the early days of the Vietnam conflict CIA agents set up their Ops in remote outposts, requisitioned private armies, overawed the superstitious natives and achieved the status of white Gods. So the context of 19th-century colonialism was briefly duplicated. That is what writing is about: time-travel. ——William Burroughs

康拉德的《黑暗之心》变成了《现代启示录》。在越南冲突早期，中情局（CIA）特工在偏远哨所建立行动点，征调私军，威慑迷信的原住民并获得“白人神祇”的地位。至此，19世纪殖民主义的语境被简短地复制了。这就是写作的本质：时间旅行。——William Burroughs

“What side?”
“On-line. From inside the system. I’m not in the meat anymore, I told you, I got out of my box.” ——Pat Cadigan

“我的肉身办不到，我没法从这一边让它运作……”
“哪一边？”
“在线。从系统内部。我不再呆在肉身里了，我告诉你了，我已经跳出了我的盒子。”——Pat Cadigan

Anti-Oedipus is an anticipatively assembled inducer for the replay of geohistoryin hypermedia， a social-systemic fast feed-forward through machinic delirium.While tracking Artaud across the plane it discovers a cosmic catatonic abstractbody that both repels its parts (deterritorializing them [from each other]) andattracts them (reterritorializing them [upon itself])， in a process that reconnectsthe parts through deterritorium as rhizomatic nets conducting schizogeneses.

《反俄狄浦斯》是一种预先装配的诱导剂，旨在通过超媒体重现地球历史，它是一种经由机器狂想实现的社会—系统性快速前馈机制。在追踪阿尔托的过程中，它发现了一种宇宙性的紧张性的抽象躯体：这种躯体既排斥其各个部分（使它们彼此去领土化），又吸引它们（将它们重新领土化于自身之上），从而以去领土化的形式重新连接各部分，形成一种根茎状网络，引导精神分裂生成。感官抵达了绝对零点。

note：从德勒兹的书进入的。安托南·阿尔托是德勒兹“无器官身体”概念的来源，兰德在这里将其描述为一个动力学过程。Cosmic catatonic abstract body就是“无器官身体”，也就是一个没有被符号化（经过俄狄浦斯）的无功能的身体躯壳，它拒绝让器官被功能化、身份化，使各部分相互脱离，又将这些碎片重新吸附到自己这个“无意义的平面”上 。

Sense reaches absolute zero.

意义到达了绝对零度。

note：也就是能指和所指断裂了，意义消失，其实我理解就是精神病状态。

The body without organs is the matter that always fills space to given degrees of intensity, and the partial objects are these degrees, these intensive parts that produce the real in space starting from matter as intensity = 0. The body without organs is the immanent substance, in the most Spinozist sense of the word; and the partial objects are like its ultimate attributes, which belong to it precisely insofar as they are really distinct and cannot on this account exclude or oppose one another.

无器官身体是始终以给定强度等级填充空间的物质，而局部客体则是这些等级，是这些从“强度=0”的物质出发、在空间中生产出“实在（the real）”的集约性部件。无器官身体是在最斯宾诺莎主义意义上的内在性实体；而局部客体则如同其终极属性，它们之所以属于该实体，恰恰是因为它们在逻辑上真实有别，且绝不会因此相互排斥或对立。

note：“强度=0”的物质比局部客体更加靠前一点，也就是纯粹的无器官身体——纯粹光滑的平面。无器官身体 (BwO) = 物质 (强度=0) = 斯宾诺莎的“实体” (Substance)；局部客体 (Partial Objects) = 强度 > 0 = 斯宾诺莎的“属性” (Attributes)。局部客体是实在界的碎片，是由冲动完全驱动的，它们在光滑平面上横冲直撞，彼此连接、断开。

Deleuze and Guattari spring schizophrenia from the grid of representation，insisting that Artaud was exploring the body. The intensive ‘infrastructure’ofevery delirium is machinery， with the body without organs as a component.

德勒兹与加塔利将“精神分裂”从表征的栅格中释放了出来，并坚持认为阿尔托是在探索身体。每一种谵妄的集约性“基础设施”都是机器，而无器官身体则是其中的一个组件。

note：无器官身体是纯粹平面，局部客体是装配在上面的没有被象征化的、完全由冲动驱动的器官（一个端口，但是没有服务在上面）。

BwO, matter degree-0 as a nonformal singularization function, is ‘not actual, but virtual-real’: spontaneous population-hyperbehaviour inducing a convergent wave which cannot be assimilated to the categories of modern (linear) science. BwOs are machinic-additional wholes or surplus products rather than logical-substitutive wholes, augmenting a multiplicity with emergent (synthetic) capabilities rather than totalizing the content of a set. This is the materialist sense of ‘system’: the exteriority of the whole to its parts with concomitant synthetic interactivity - real influence rather than generic representation.

无器官身体（BwO），即作为非形式化奇异化函数的“物质度-0”，它是“非现实的，却是虚拟-实在的”：它是诱导出一场会聚波的自发种群超行为，而这场波无法被同化进现代（线性）科学的范畴中。无器官身体是机器性加算式的整体或剩余产物，而非逻辑替代式的整体；它以突现的（综合的）能力来增强流溢的多样性，而非将一个集合的内容总体化。这就是“系统”的唯物主义含义：整体对其部件的外部性，以及随之而来的综合交互性——即真实的物理影响，而非概括性的表征。

Cybernetics folds pragmatism into involutionary technical runaway.

Punk arises within the culture of universal prostitution and laughs at the deathof the social.

控制论将实用主义折叠进一种内旋式的技术失控之中。

朋克兴起于普遍娼妓化的文化之中，并对“社会性”之死发出冷笑。

note：技术的发展不是为了解决人类问题（外延式的进化），而是向内坍缩、自我纠缠。

‘No longer resisting the flow of events or pretending to chart a course throughthem’，P cyberpunk soaks up the worst from both. Its compulsive migrations intocomputer systems register a desperate scrabbling to escape from the clumsilyunderdesigned， theopolitically mutilated， techno-industrially pressure-cookedand data-baked， retrovirally diseased， tortured， shredded zombie meat. This is nolonger a departure from matter in the direction of spirit or the Ideas where theself will find its home， but a dismantling of the self within a machinic matrix：not disembodied but disorganized. An out to body experience.

“不再抵抗事件的洪流，亦不再幻想能从中开辟航向”，赛博朋克将两者的最恶劣之处悉数吸纳。它那强迫性地向计算机系统的迁徙，记录了一场绝望的攀爬，旨在逃离那笨拙且设计低劣的、神权政治式残缺的、技术-工业高压锅式烹煮且数据烘烤的、逆转录病毒式病变的、备受折磨且被粉碎的僵尸烂肉。这不再是那种背离物质、投向精神或“理念”——即让自我找寻家园的启程，而是在机器矩阵中对自我进行的拆解：并非脱离肉体（disembodied），而是脱离组织（disorganized）。这是一场“向躯体外”的体验（An out to body experience）。

The machinic unconscious tends only to flee， across a primary-processtopography that is shaped by pain-gradients and escape thresholds. Whatregisters for the secondary process as memory， experience， data-acquisition， isfor the primary process， scarring， damage， sticky microsofted irritations.

机器无意识倾向于仅仅去逃逸，横跨在那由痛觉梯度和逃逸阈值所塑造的原初过程地势之上。在次级阶段看来是记忆、经验、数据采集的东西，在原初阶段看来，则是结疤、损害、以及黏糊糊的、“微软化”的刺激。

As matter-energy flows are captured by attractors the BwO is stratified asmacro-and micro-organisms. ‘Every coupling of machines， every production of amachine， every sound of a machine running， has become unbearable to the bodywithout organs. Beneath the organs it senses larvae and disgusting worms， andthe action of a God who botches or strangles it through organization’.-Gatheringin the tributary attractor basins of social megamachinery， fluctuations are case-packed into reproducible units- geochemical， bio-organic， cultural - encrustedwithin security pods.

当物质-能量流被引力子捕获时，无器官身体便被分层为宏观与微观的有机体。“机器的每一次耦合，机器的每一次生产，机器运行的每一声轰鸣，对于无器官身体而言都已变得难以忍受。在器官之下，它感知到了蛆虫与令人作呕的蠕虫，以及一个通过‘组织化’将其拙劣修补或扼杀的上帝之举。”——在社会巨型机器那分支汇流的引力子盆地中，种种波动被装箱成可复制的单元——地理化学的、生物有机性的、文化性的——并被包裹在安全舱（Security pods）的硬壳之中。

Oedipus is a box at the end of the world， glued to the monitor， watching it allcome apart.

俄狄浦斯是世界尽头的一个箱子，它紧贴着显示器，注视着这一切分崩离析。

The horror.

令人恐惧。

The heart of darkness spins narrative from durations of waiting to get there. Ihad plenty of time for meditation’mutters Marlow， ‘… now and then I wouldgive some thought to Kurtz’.

黑暗之心从抵达前的漫长等待中纺织出叙事。“我有足够的时间进行冥想，”马洛咕哝道，“……不时地，我会给库尔茨留出一些思绪。”

When you try to visualize Kurtz nothing comes except a shape obliteratinglight， something dark and complicated， like a giant spider， waiting at the end ofthe river， drawing you up to it. Somewhere far back - at an indiscernible pointclosing down a fantasy of innocent sunlight - a threshold was crossed， and youstrayed into the web.

当你试图勾勒库尔茨的轮廓，浮现的只有一种抹杀光线的形状，某种阴暗而复杂的东西，如同一只盘踞在河流尽头的巨蛛，正将你引向它。在遥远的后方——在某个闭合了纯真阳光幻梦的模糊点位上——一道阈值已被跨越，而你已误入蛛网。

With each telling of the story Kurtz becomes colder， darker， more inevitable，fatally anticipating k.virus catastrophe， as if a tendril of tomorrow wereburrowing back. What has he found among these African or Cambodianaboriginals， with their ‘faces like grotesque masks’?9 There are reports ofmilitary bestiality， butchery， carnage， head-hunting， collecting ears， severing thevaccinated arms from children. The Kurtz-process masks itself in wolf-pelts ofregression， as if returning to the repressed， discovering a lost truth， excavatingthe fossils of monsters.

随着故事的每一次讲述，库尔茨变得愈发冰冷、阴暗且不可避免，他致命地预示着‘K-病毒’灾难，仿佛一根未来的触须正向后钻探。在那些有着‘如荒诞面具般面孔’的非洲或柬埔寨原住民中，他究竟发现了什么？流传着种种关于军事兽行、屠杀、血洗、猎头、割耳，以及砍掉孩子接种过疫苗的手臂的报告。‘库尔茨-进程’将自身伪装在‘退行’的狼皮之下，仿佛是在回归被压抑之物，发现失落的真理，或是挖掘怪物的化石。

Going up that river was like travelling back to the earliest beginnings of theworld， when vegetation rioted on the earth and the big trees were kings. Anempty stream， a great silence， an impenetrable forest. The air was warm，thick， heavy， sluggish … The long stretches of waterway ran on， deserted，into the gloom of overshadowed distances … We were wanderers onprehistoric earth， on an earth that wore the aspect of an unknown planet.

溯流而上，如同回溯至世界最初的起点，那时植被在地球上疯狂滋长，参天巨树称王。空旷的溪流，死一般的寂静，一片无法穿透的森林。空气温暖、浓稠、沉重、迟滞……长长的水道蜿蜒而去，荒无人烟，隐入那阴影笼罩的幽暗远方……我们是史前地球上的流浪者，这地球呈现出一颗未知行星的面貌。

Territorial production codes by deterritorializing； unfixing by hunter-gathering，according to a cold or metastatic cultural code that equilibriates on a (Bateson)’plateau’. Earth begins its migration-in-place towards the globe.

领土通过去领土化来进行生产编码；依照某种在（贝特森式）‘高原’上达成平衡的、冷酷或转移性的文化编码，通过狩猎-采集来实现去固定化。地球开始了它那向着‘全球’迈进的就地迁徙。

The earth is the primitive， savage unity of desire and production … thegreat unengendered stasis … quasi-cause of production and the object ofdesire (it is on the earth that desire becomes bound to its own repression)…：The primitive territorial machine， with its immobile motor， the earth， isalready a socialmachine， a megamachine， that codes the flows ofproduction.

大地是欲望与生产那原始、野蛮的统一体……是那伟大的、非生而成的静止态……是生产的准原因,
与欲望的对象（正是在大地上，欲望变得与其自身的压抑紧密相连）……：这台拥有‘大地’这一不动机器的原始领土机器，本身便已是一台社会机器，一台对生产流进行编码的巨型机器。

Coding the body pins it out in extension， conducting descendency away from thegermo-somatic ‘meat circuit’l and its cyberplexive tangles. The social orsomatic being is forbidden from being meat (disinherited animal tissuesimultaneous with fate， spontaneous， orphan and mutable matter) and is borneinstead towards the humanity of the organic self or body-for-itself； acorporealized person who is born， lives and dies.

对躯体进行编码，将其钉死在广延之中，使谱系传导远离那生殖-躯体的“肉身回路”及其赛博丛结的缠绕。这种社会性或躯体性的存在被禁止成为“肉身”——即那种与命运同步、自发的、孤儿式的、易变的、被剥夺了继承权的动物组织；相反，它被推向了有机自我或“自为之身”的人性；成为一个具身化的、经历出生、生活与死亡的人

note：这里使用广延说明身体被象征化到了空间中，兰德在此暗示，编码过程将原本充满强度的、不可分的肉体转化为空间中可度量、可管理的“物体” 。被禁止成为“肉身”：其实就是必须进入象征界，必须进入俄狄浦斯阶段。这里也反讽了萨特式的存在主义，兰德认为这种“自为”其实是一种被困在“人性”框架里的幻象 。

Man must constitute himself through the repression of the intense germinal influx, the great biocosmic memory that threatens to deluge every attempt at collectivity.

人必须通过压抑那强烈的、胚芽般的涌流来构筑自身；那是伟大的生物宇宙记忆，它威胁着要淹没任何建立集体性的尝试。

note：构建自身其实就是分离，分离了才能开始进入象征界，抵抗实在界的涌流。

Incest and cannibalism are proscribed loops, short-circuits, the avatars of a delirium indifferent to persons which the codes must segregate; condensing a totemic social order protected by taboo. Aboriginal codes ritualistically constitute a somatic realm of ancestrally invested bodies and cooked meat, immunizing it against uncoded tracts populated by enemies, prey animals, unsettled spirits, magical plants and unprocessed corpses.

乱伦与食人是受禁的回路与短路，是某种对“人格”漠不关心的谵妄化身，各种代码必须将其隔离 ；以此凝聚起一个受禁忌保护的图腾式社会秩序 。原始代码通过仪式构筑起一个躯体领域，其间充满了祖先注资的身体与经过烹饪的肉，从而使其免疫于那些由敌人、猎物、不安的灵魂、魔幻植物和未经处理的尸体所占据的、未被编码的荒野。

Arriving reprocessed from inexistence at phase-transition into Hell or the future, you slide an interlock-pin into its sub-cortical socket, shifting to the other side of the screen (coma-zoned infotech undeath). Pandemonium scrolls out in silence. Decayed pixel-dust drifts into grey dunes. (Didn’t anyone tell you not to play with the switches?)

你从“不存在”中被重新处理，在进入地狱或未来的相变点抵达；你将一枚联锁销滑入皮层下的插槽，向着屏幕的另一端移位（那是处于昏迷地带的信息技术不死状态）。万魔殿在沉寂中滚屏而出。腐朽的像素尘埃漂移成灰色的沙丘。（难道没有人告诉你，不要乱动开关吗？）

The function of shamanism is to implement what is forbidden, exactly and comprehensively as and why it is forbidden, but in specially segregated compart­ments of the socius, where it provides a metacoding apparatus, meticulously quarantined against ‘the transmissibility of taboo’14 with its ‘power of infection or contagion’.15 It enables the codes of the primitive socius to operate upon themselves, to monitor and adjust themselves, according to a secondary regulation that is repressed in general even whilst it is encouraged in particular. An epidemic shamanism – feeding all the codes back upon themselves – threatens absolute social disaster.

萨满教的功能在于执行那些被禁止之事——精准且全面地按照其被禁的原因与方式去执行——但这种执行被限制在社会机体中特别隔离的舱室里。在此，它提供了一种元编码装置，并针对具有“传染或扩散力量”的“禁忌可传播性”进行了细致的检疫隔离。它使原始社会的代码能够作用于自身，进行自我监控与调整，这种调整遵循着一种在总体上被压抑、却在特定情况下被鼓励的次级规制。一种“流行病式”的萨满教——即将所有代码反馈回自身——威胁着要引发绝对的社会灾难。

note：有点像原父。

The meaning of ‘taboo’, as we see it, diverges in two contrary directions. To us it means on the one hand, ‘sacred’, ‘consecrated’, and on the other ‘uncanny’, ‘dangerous’, ‘forbidden’, ‘unclean’.

正如我们所见，“禁忌”（taboo）的含义向两个相反的方向分叉。对我们而言，它一方面意味着“神圣的”、“神圣化的”；而另一方面则意味着“怪诞的”（uncanny）、“危险的”、“被禁止的”以及“不洁的”。

库尔兹践行着精神分裂分析（schizoanalysis），陷入阴影之中，变得无法察觉。最新的照片通过黑暗抹除了他的面孔，人格被那空白的战争源头所遮蔽。他偏好的运作模式是快速的（断开）连接（即：打了就跑）。敌方情报的渗透已被封死。数据熄灭，伴随着些许有失外交水准的鲜血。情况看起来很糟（如果这看起来还算是个情况的话）。这一进程已走向土著化（native），正逼近游牧暴动的“饱和零点”，并与无器官身体（BwO）建立了连接。库尔兹至少和威拉德一样清楚：查理（越共）眼中的“绝佳休整”就是“冷米饭加一点鼠肉”。他正变得比越南人更像越南人。

一切都走向毁灭（Everything goes to hell）。

vr was a medico-military computer application before arriving in the mass entertainment market. It is first a technics of perception, and only derivatively a medium for immersive hallucination. If artificial space substitutes an ideal body-image for a ‘real’ one, it is only because it first invades the real (imageless) body. Virtual technics deflects reality, rather than cancelling or eclipsing it. Matter as the intensity of the circuit, not the adequacy of the representation.

在进入大众娱乐市场之前，VR 最初是医疗-军事领域的计算机应用。它首先是一项感知的技术（technics of perception），其次才衍生为一种沉浸式幻觉的媒介。如果人造空间用一个理想的躯体图像取代了“真实的”躯体图像，那仅仅是因为它首先入侵了真实的（无图像的）身体。虚拟技术是偏转（deflects）了现实，而非取消或遮蔽现实。物质被视为电路的强度，而非表象的充实。

Evening at the end of the river: thick tropical heat, an airstrike coming in, and Morrison is sliding through oedipal murder and incest into the occult sonics of matricide. Kurtz waits in the foetid gloom, ready to die. His guerrillas are preparing to slaughter a water-buffalo below, laughing and clapping among torches, automatic rifles and shrunken heads. Patches of mottled technoderm woven with electronics are emerging. Daddy and mummy means nothing anymore. You scrape away your face and step into the dark …

河流尽头的黄昏：稠密的丛林酷热，空袭即将来临，莫里森（Jim Morrison）的歌声正滑过俄狄浦斯式的谋杀与乱伦，坠入弑母（matricide）的奥秘音响之中。库尔兹在恶臭的幽暗中等待，准备赴死。他的游击队员们正在下方准备屠宰一头水牛，在火把、自动步枪和干制首级之间大笑着、拍着手。你左手握着一把 28 厘米的锯齿战斗刀。“威拉德”这层皮正成碎片地剥落，暴露出某种超越男性气概、超越人性、超越生命的东西。交织着电子元件、带有斑驳色泽的技术皮肤（technoderm）正隐约浮现。爹地和妈地不再有任何意义。你刮掉自己的脸，步入黑暗之中……
